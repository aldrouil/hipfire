Resident-lowered passback evidence packaging — 2026-10-18

Versioned compact evidence includes the native A/B JSON/sample arrays, process
metadata and daemon proofs, oracle stdout and per-arm manifests/child logs,
serve-smoke receipts, build logs (including the failed first test build), source
patch/provenance, the predeclared protocol, analysis and raw-archive checksums.
Ignored *.log receipts are explicitly included; blanket staging is not used.

The two large raw-state archives are preserved locally alongside this file but
are deliberately NOT Git objects (approximately 1.8 GB combined):
  ORACLE-dumps-run1.tar.zst
  ORACLE-dumps-twin.tar.zst
Their SHA-256 values are recorded in ORACLE-dumps.sha256. Neither the archives
nor the original /tmp dump roots were deleted. No remote artifact publication
or upload was performed. A fresh Git checkout therefore contains manifests,
logs and reproduction instructions, not raw tensor bytes; rerun the ignored
hardware oracle on the documented fixture or obtain the archives out of band.
A checksum alone is not a downloadable archive.

The originally copied compact share-zero directories used hand/mixed names and
were empty because that case actually names its arms cpu/passback0. Additional
unaltered manifests and child logs were preserved from the original twin dump
under ORACLE-receipts/run572723/cut16_share0_nb0_{cpu,passback0}/. No new model
execution, measurement or numerical analysis was performed for this packaging.
